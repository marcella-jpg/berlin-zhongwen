📝 Lesson Notes & Audio Tips

Grammar from last session

  • 1. Word order is Subject + Verb + Object.
    我喝水。 Wǒ hē shuǐ.
    I drink water.
  • 2. 想 + verb = “want to …”
    我想吃米饭。 Wǒ xiǎng chī mǐfàn.
    I want to eat rice.
  • 3. Add 吗 to make a yes/no question.
    你喝茶吗？ Nǐ hē chá ma?
    Do you drink tea?

🎧 Audio tips

  • Shadow it. Play a word, pause, and repeat it right away. Copy the pitch, not just the sounds.
  • Mind the tones. 水 shuǐ dips down then up (3rd tone). 茶 chá rises like a question (2nd tone).
  • Go slow, then fast. Say each sentence slowly three times, then at normal speed.
  • Hide the Pinyin. Try the cards with Pinyin off and see how many you recognize.
